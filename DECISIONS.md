# Product & Architecture Decisions (`DECISIONS.md`)

## Implemented Guardrails

1. **Integer Kobo Monetary Arithmetic (`src/lib/money.ts`)**
   - All monetary values are stored and calculated in integer minor units (kobo) and formatted to `NGN` (`₦`) only at the display boundary. Binary floating-point math is never used for authoritative totals.
2. **Non-Custodial Recordkeeping Boundary**
   - Tracking accounts (`GTBank ••2041`, `POS settlement`, `Cash`) are explicitly presented as bookkeeping labels maintained by the business—not live bank connections or wallets.
   - Invoices and supplier bills record commercial claims and bookkeeping payment statuses; Ledgerly never initiates transfers or holds funds.
   - Invoices carry an explicit notice distinguishing ordinary Ledgerly invoices from fiscally certified NRS e-invoices.
3. **Append-Only Posted Records & Traceable Reversals (`src/lib/domain.ts`)**
   - Posted transactions cannot be overwritten or deleted. Corrections require a reason, mark the original record as `reversed`, post an offsetting reversal entry (and optional replacement entry), and record a before/after snapshot in the audit feed.
4. **Deterministic Money Brief (`buildMoneyBrief`)**
   - Observations are generated by transparent, deterministic rules (overdue receivables, obligations due within 7 days, low-cash threshold, and period cash movement) with one-click drill-down to contributing records.
5. **Server & Client Role-Based Access Control (`src/lib/tenant.ts`, `src/app/api/v1/workspace/route.ts`)**
   - Enforces `owner`, `admin`, `bookkeeper`, and read-only `accountant` capabilities. The `Staff` role remains gated per PRD Section 7 until task-scoped record restrictions are enabled.
